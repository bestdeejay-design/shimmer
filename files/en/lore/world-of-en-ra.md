# World of En-Ra
## Home of the Stream

> They say En-Ra took his first steps in the Stream
> at sunset, when the shadows grew long
> and the air turned golden.
> And the world around him stood still.

---

## 1. ABOUT THE WORLD

The world where En-Ra was born — the first world to hear the Stream. Here humanity first consciously entered the Psi-Stream, here the first schools were founded, here the age of the Architects began.

The world of En-Ra is no capital, no centre of the galaxy. It is unremarkable from the outside. But it was here that the Stream 'spoke' for the first time.

### 1.1. Geography

The world of En-Ra is an Earth-type planet:
- Three continents, divided by an ocean.
- Temperate climate for most of the year.
- Solitary mountain ranges on the eastern continent.

Nothing special. An ordinary world. There are thousands like it.

But it was on the eastern continent, in a quiet mountain valley, that En-Ra first heard the Stream.

### 1.2. The Echo of the First Hour

The world has changed since then. Not physically — energetically. The Stream is denser here than in any other world. The air feels 'thick', colours are brighter, dreams more real.

Even now, 11,000 years later, in the world of En-Ra you can:
- See the shadows of the past — the Shimmers of the First Hour, still present.
- Hear the echo of the first voice — if you know how to listen.
- Feel what the Stream was like when it was first born.

---

## 2. WHAT REMAINS

### 2.1. The Great Vault

A repository of knowledge, created by Sira-Nam (3rd Hour). Located in the world of En-Ra — the heart of archiving.

The Great Vault is not a library in the ordinary sense. It is a mental structure — a map of all knowledge ever recorded in the Stream.

To read a book from the Great Vault, you need not take it in your hands. You need only attune to its frequency.

After the Sundering, access to the Great Vault is limited — local only, within the world of En-Ra.

### 2.2. The School of the First Step

The place where En-Ra taught his first students. Not a building — a valley, an open amphitheatre surrounded by mountains.

Here, to this day:
- The 'trace' of En-Ra's voice can still be heard — a barely perceptible vibration in the Stream.
- Every stone 'remembers' the first lessons.
- At night you can see the Shimmers of students — they return here, even after millennia.

### 2.3. The Garden of Silence

A place where one goes to stop hearing the Stream. Created as a refuge for those who cannot bear the density of the Stream in the world of En-Ra.

Silence in the literal sense: in the Garden, the Stream 'retreats.' A person is left alone with themselves — without the background noise of others' thoughts.

The Garden is located on a plateau high in the mountains. The entrance is guarded by obelisks — slender, tall, with barely visible engravings.

---

## 3. THE WORLD AFTER THE SUNDERING

The world of En-Ra is one of the few that retained its connection to the Stream after the Sundering. Why?

Three reasons:
- **Density**: The Stream is too dense here to disappear completely. It 'survived' by inertia.
- **Memory**: Too many Shimmers settled here over 10,000 years.
- **Schools**: An unbroken tradition — people continued to learn to hear the Stream, generation after generation.

Now the world of En-Ra is a quiet, peaceful planet. The Stream works, but locally. No one leaves the world — and no one arrives. The inhabitants know of other worlds but cannot reach them.
