# The Shimmer
## Nature, mechanics, galactic clock

> The Shimmer is not life after death.
> It is continuation. Consciousness does not die — it sounds.
> The only question is who will hear it.

---

## 1. WHAT IS THE SHIMMER

**The Shimmer** is the state of consciousness after physical death in which the person continues to exist within the Psi-Stream.

It is not an afterlife. Not heaven. Not reincarnation. It is the **trace** that consciousness leaves in the living fabric of the Stream.

### 1.1. What it looks like

From the outside: a person has died — the body has stopped breathing.
From the inside: the person did not notice the transition. They are still "here" — only the body is no longer required. They can see, hear, feel — not through organs, but directly, through the Stream.

A shimmering consciousness is like a candle flame in total darkness. It does not depart — it continues to burn as long as there is fuel.

### 1.2. Fuel of the Shimmer

What sustains the Shimmer:
- **Memories** — the richer the life, the longer the Shimmer.
- **Love** — being remembered by connected consciousnesses nourishes it.
- **Will** — the desire to remain. Some consciousnesses "hold on" longer because they do not wish to fade.

---

## 2. DURATION OF THE SHIMMER

### 2.1. Ordinary Shimmer

An ordinary person shimmers for **exactly as long as their Galactic Hour lasts** — 1,000 years.

After 1,000 years, their Shimmer fades. It does not disappear — it passes into the depth of the Stream.

### 2.2. Architect's Shimmer

An Architect shimmers **brighter and longer**. Their consciousness is a beacon. They can consciously interact with the living, guide, influence the Stream — for as long as their Hour lasts.

### 2.3. Return after 12,000 years

When the galaxy completes a full cycle (12,000 years) and returns to the same phase — **every Shimmer of that Hour resumes**.

A person who died 12,000 years ago is heard again in the Stream — together with all who shimmered in their Hour. Their voices become audible once more.

This is not reincarnation (the soul does not migrate into a new body). It is **resonance** — time returns to the same turn of the spiral, and everything that sounded then sounds again.

---

## 3. THE GALACTIC CLOCK

### 3.1. Mechanics

The galaxy rotates around its centre. One full revolution — 12,000 years. This revolution is divided into 12 equal parts — **Galactic Hours** — of 1,000 years each.

The Psi-Stream responds to this rotation as the ocean responds to the Moon:
- Each Hour has its own "tide" of the Stream.
- Each Hour births its own Architect — the one who sounds loudest in that particular segment of time.

### 3.2. Why 1,000 years

1,000 years is not a random number. It is the time in which the collective consciousness of humanity can:
- Attune to a new Architect.
- Realise their discovery.
- Prepare for the next.

## 4. THE TRANSITION INTO SHIMMER

### 4.1. In Lira's world

Passing into the Shimmer is not a tragedy. It is the next stage.

A person, feeling the approach of the body's end, prepares:
- Says goodbye to loved ones (not forever — they will hear each other in the Stream).
- Passes on affairs, knowledge, unfinished dreams.
- In the final moment — relaxes and "breathes" themselves into the Stream.

After the transition, their consciousness continues to live — without a body, without pain, without time.

### 4.2. In Kai's world

The Shimmer is a forgotten knowledge. People truly die. For them, death is the end. The body is buried in the sand, and within a generation no one remembers the person.

This is why it is so hard for Kai to accept his dreams — in his world, there is no concept of "life after death." Everything he sees is either madness, or a revelation with no explanation.

---

## 5. THE ARCHITECT AND THE SHIMMER

An Architect in the Shimmer is not a passive consciousness. They can:
- **Influence the Stream**: set the tone, strengthen or weaken resonance.
- **Communicate with the living**: through dreams, visions, artefacts.
- **Create**: Lira, already in the Shimmer, created hundreds of Spires and artefact-paths.
- **Wait**: an Architect can consciously delay their fading in order to await a successor.

But this demands colossal effort. Every act of creation in the Shimmer shortens its duration.

Lira spent nearly 1,000 years of her Shimmer building the Path for Kai. She gave the Stream almost everything she had — so that he would make it in time.

---

## 6. TYPES OF SHIMMER

### 6.1. Fresh Shimmer

The first 10–20 years after death. Consciousness still "remembers" the body — it can interact with the physical world almost as if alive.

### 6.2. Mature Shimmer

10–500 years. Consciousness has grown accustomed to existence without a body. It can create, communicate, influence. The period of greatest strength.

### 6.3. Fading Shimmer

500–1,000 years. Consciousness begins to "dim." The connection to the physical world weakens. Thoughts grow slower. Lira is in this phase.

### 6.4. Deep Shimmer (Dormancy)

After 1,000 years. Consciousness ceases to be active. It "sleeps" in the depth of the Stream, awaiting the next turn of the galactic cycle.

### 6.5. Returned Shimmer

After 12,000 years, when the cycle repeats. Consciousness hears everyone in its Hour — and sounds again, together with all who shimmered in its Hour.

---

## 7. WHAT A SHIMMERER FEELS

The Shimmer is not "life in heaven." It is a complex, multifaceted state:

- **Peace**: no body — no pain, hunger, fear. But also no joy of touch.
- **Loneliness**: you hear everyone, but no one hears you unless they know how to listen.
- **Timelessness**: past, present and future exist simultaneously. You can "review" your life — but you cannot change it.
- **Longing for the living**: shimmerers miss the physical world — the wind, the warmth of a hand, the taste of water.

This is why the meeting of Kai and Lira in the Shimmer is so important. Two shimmering consciousnesses that found each other — this is the rarest thing. And the greatest happiness possible in the Stream.
