# World of Kai
## The Lost World

> This is a world that forgot the Stream.
> It did not turn away — it forgot.
> As a person forgets that they once knew how to fly in dreams.

---

## 1. THE WORLD BEFORE THE SUNDERING

Before the Sundering, the world of Kai was an ordinary world in the galactic network. Not a capital, not a centre — but not the outskirts either. Moderate Stream density, stable threads to neighbouring worlds.

People lived here. The Stream worked. Children were born with background access — like everyone else. Nothing special — just a world, one of thousands.

Approximately 6,000 years ago, the world of Kai was cut off from the network. The Sundering happened abruptly — not a gradual fading, but a severing of connection with the rest of the galaxy.

Why this happened is unknown. There are many theories, no answer.

### 1.1. The Disconnection

After the severing of the external threads, the Stream in the world of Kai did not vanish instantly. It faded gradually — like the sound of a bell dying away minute by minute.

The first generation after the Sundering:
- Still remembered how to use the Stream.
- Tried to maintain the technology.
- Passed the knowledge to their children.

The second generation:
- The Stream grew 'quieter.' Children heard it less well.
- Complex technologies stopped working.
- Only artefacts remained — like battery-powered radio stations.

The third generation:
- The Stream is a myth. 'It was, once upon a time.'
- Artefacts are 'ancestors' magic.'
- A handful still feel it — but take it for imagination.

Two hundred years after the Sundering, the world of Kai became what we know it as now.

## 2. THE WORLD NOW

### 2.1. The Physical World

The world of Kai is a desert planet.
- Most of the surface is sand and bare rock.
- Oases are rare — life is concentrated around water sources.
- The climate is harsh: heat by day, cold by night.
- The wind is the constant soundscape of the world.

### 2.2. Settlements

People live in small communities. The largest are oasis-cities. Each such city is a fortress with towers, wells, and narrow streets planned to hold the shade.

Life is hard, but not hopeless. People have grown accustomed.

---

## 3. ATTITUDE TOWARD THE STREAM

### 3.1. Official Position

The Stream does not exist. It is 'children's tales' and 'ancestral superstitions.'

No temples of the Stream. No schools. No teachers.

### 3.2. Folk Memory

But among the people, these live on:
- **Legends** — of 'the time when the world sang.'
- **Signs** — 'if the wind dies before dawn, expect news.'
- **Fears** — 'do not look at the stars too long, they will call to you.'

### 3.3. Those Who Remember

A rare few — born with background access. They feel the Stream but do not know what it is. For them, it is:
- Dreams that are too vivid.
- An inexplicable longing for something that does not exist in this world.
- An ability to sense where water is — a 'nose for oases.'
- A feeling that there is something more beyond this world.

One in a million. Kai is one of them.

---

## 4. ARTEFACTS OF THE PAST

### 4.1. What Has Survived

In the world of Kai, artefacts from the age of the Stream have survived:
- **The Silent Walls** — ruins of an ancient city. A faint current of the Stream still 'flows' within the walls. Those who approach hear a whisper.
- **The Garden of Stones** — a place where knowledge is 'recorded' in the stones. No one knows how to read them, but the stones are warm to the touch in any season.
- **The Temple of No Voice** — a ruined temple where people once learned to hear the Stream. Now — a heap of stones and sand.
- **Crystals** — rare, but still active. If you touch one, you can see a flash of another's memory.

### 4.2. Attitude Toward Artefacts

Officially — 'dangerous relics of the past.' In reality — they are studied in secret, collected, prayed upon.

Artefact traders are a separate caste. They know where to search, how to excavate, and whom to sell to.

---

## 5. KAI IN THIS WORLD

Kai was born in a small oasis-city by the eastern cliffs.

He:
- Had dreams since childhood that he could not explain.
- Felt things that were not there — and it frightened those around him.
- Was a 'strange child' — too quiet, too thoughtful.
- Found a crystal — and the world was never the same.

The world of Kai is not ready for who Kai will become. But Kai is not ready for who he must become.

Their meeting — the meeting of two realities alien to each other — will be inevitable.
