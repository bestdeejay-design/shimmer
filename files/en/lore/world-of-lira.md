# World of Lira
# Bridge Between Eras

> Lira did not choose her Hour.
> The Hour chose her.
> And she accepted it — together with the silence
> that was to come.

---

## 1. THE WORLD IN LIRA'S TIME

### 1.1. The Tenth Hour

When Lira was born, the galaxy was already sundered. The Ninth Hour had ended with the Sundering — the severing of connections between worlds.

The world of Lira found itself cut off from the external network. The Stream worked — but locally. Only one's own world could be 'heard.'

Lira was born into a world that remembered greatness and lost it.

### 1.2. The Remembering World

The world of Lira differs fundamentally from the world of Kai:

**World of Lira:**
- The Stream is active (locally).
- Knowledge of the Stream is preserved.
- The Architects are part of history.
- Stream technologies work — within the world.

**World of Kai:**
- The Stream is forgotten.
- Knowledge is lost.
- The Architects are a myth.
- The technology is 'ancestors' magic.'

The world of Lira is **the remembering one**. It did not lose its connection to the Stream. It lost its connection to the rest of the galaxy.

---

## 2. GEOGRAPHY

The world of Lira is a green planet.
- Seas, forests, fertile plains.
- Mild climate for most of the year.
- Long coastlines.

Nature here is not a backdrop. It is active. The forests remember, the rivers sing, the wind carries not only scents but also echoes of others' thoughts.

### 2.1. The Spires

The Spires are the hallmark of the world of Lira. Three towers, built by her own hand.

Each Spire is not merely a building. It is:
- A receiver and transmitter of the Stream.
- An anchor for the threads of the Stream.
- A beacon for those seeking the way.

The Spires are positioned at key points of the world, forming a triangle. Within this triangle, the Stream is especially dense.

After Lira's departure into Shimmer, the Spires continue to function. They are her legacy, her voice that never falls silent.

---

## 3. LIRA'S LEGACY

### 3.1. What She Left Behind

1. **The Spires** — three towers functioning as transceivers.
2. **Path Artefacts** — 'keys' for the next Architect.
3. **The Garden of Stones** — a repository of knowledge and instructions.
4. **The Path** — a physical route from the world of Lira to the world of Kai.

### 3.2. The Garden of Stones

Located in the world of Lira. Created after Lira learned of Kai's existence.

The Garden of Stones is not a garden. It is a space where memories, knowledge, and keys that Kai will need are 'recorded' in the stones.

The stones are arranged in a circle. At the centre — emptiness. A place for the one who will come.

---

## 4. THE WORLD AFTER LIRA

After Lira's departure into Shimmer, the world did not empty — it continues to live. But:

- There is no new Architect (the next will be born in the world of Kai, which is cut off).
- Stream technologies are maintained by inertia.
- The Spires function, but without an Architect their power diminishes.

The world waits. The people know that one day the connection will be restored. They do not know when. But they believe.

---

## 5. THE PATH TO KAI

The Path is Lira's most important project. A physical route connecting two worlds separated by:

- Space (an unknown distance).
- The state of the Stream (one world remembers, the other has forgotten).
- Time (11,000 years of galactic history).

The Path is not a portal. It is a real route that can be traversed physically. Lira created it while in Shimmer — spending nearly all of her Shimmer to do so.

The Path begins in the world of Lira and ends in the world of Kai.

The exit of the Path in the world of Kai is the **Tomb of the First Ones**. Kai will find it. Lira knows.

---

## 6. WHY LIRA CHOSE KAI

Lira did not choose Kai. **The Stream chose.** But Lira accepted the choice.

She knew:
- That her Hour was the last before the silence.
- That the next Architect would be born in a world that had lost the Stream.
- That he would be alone — without teachers, without schools, without tradition.
- That he would need a path — not only physical, but mental.

Lira could have spent the remainder of her Shimmer in peace. Instead, she spent it preparing a path for one she would never see alive.

She did it not for glory. Not for remembrance. But because she **could not do otherwise**.

Because the Architect's purpose is not to build. To archive. To foresee. To guide.

**And if need be — to shimmer long enough so that the one who follows does not lose their way in the dark.**
