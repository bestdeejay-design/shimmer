# Timeline
## History of the galaxy in dates and events

> Time is not a line.
> It is a spiral. Each turn resembles the one before,
> but never repeats it.
> And at the centre of the spiral — the Stream.

---

## 1. BEFORE THE BEGINNING — "The Silent Ocean"

**When:** The dark ages, long before the first Architect.

The Psi-Stream always existed. It was like an ocean without wind — calm, full of voices that sounded, but to which no one could answer. People were born, lived, died, their consciousnesses flowed into the Stream — but no one knew how to listen consciously.

This period lasted hundreds of thousands of years. Sometimes there were "spontaneous resonances" — when entire peoples simultaneously believed the same thing and the Stream answered with reality. That was how myths, legends, "miracles" were born. But no one understood how it worked.

The silence before the first voice.

---

## 2. THE FIRST HOUR — En-Ra

**When:** 0–1,000 years of the Galactic Clock (GC)

**Key event:** En-Ra consciously enters the Psi-Stream for the first time.

En-Ra opened the door. He did not build it — he opened it. A door that was always there — only no one knew it was a door.

**What happened:**
- Humanity heard the Stream consciously for the first time.
- Teaching began — En-Ra taught listening, speaking, resonating.
- The first "schools of the Stream" were created — not buildings, but mental structures.

**Architect:** En-Ra (lived 200 years, then passed into the Shimmer).

**Outcome of the Hour:** Humanity received the gift of hearing. No longer blind, feeling the sun on their skin — sighted.

---

## 3. THE SECOND HOUR — Ki-El

**When:** 1,000–2,000 years GC

**Key event:** Creation of the first Path Artifacts.

Ki-El — a seer. He saw that the connection between worlds could break. And he began building bridges.

**What happened:**
- Creation of Path Artifacts — tools that store the Stream's "anchors."
- First attempts to record knowledge into the Stream (proto-archiving).
- Ki-El predicts the Severance — a time when the Stream would grow "quiet."

**Architect:** Ki-El.

**Outcome of the Hour:** The first "insurance" — if the Stream ever fell silent, the beacons would remain.

---

## 4. THE THIRD HOUR — Sira-Nam

**When:** 2,000–3,000 years GC

**Key event:** Creation of the Psi-Archiving system.

Sira-Nam turned scattered knowledge into structure.

**What happened:**
- The Great Codex was created — the first map of the Stream.
- Systematisation of knowledge: history, technology, arts.
- Archiving — the ability to "record" information directly into the Stream.

**Architect:** Sira-Nam.

**Outcome of the Hour:** Humanity no longer lost knowledge. All that was learned — was preserved.

---

## 5. THE FOURTH HOUR — Tal-Mor

**When:** 3,000–4,000 years GC

**Key event:** Beginning of the construction of the Path.

Tal-Mor begins building the Path — a physical route through space. He does not yet know why — he simply "sees" that one day it will be needed.

**What happened:**
- The Path: first segments between the nearest worlds.
- Path Artifacts: "anchors" placed along the route.
- Tal-Mor records a prophecy: "When the Stream falls silent, walk by the stones."

**Architect:** Tal-Mor.

**Outcome of the Hour:** The first physical connection between worlds — a path that can be walked even when the Stream is silent.

---

## 6. THE FIFTH — SEVENTH HOURS

**When:** 4,000–8,000 years GC

**Key event:** The flourishing of the Stream civilisation. The era of world-building.

The next three Hours — a period when humanity spread across the galaxy. Each Hour brought new Architects who pushed the boundaries of the possible.

The names of these Architects are partially lost. It is known that during this period:
- Dozens of habitable worlds were created.
- Psi-Terraforming technologies were developed.
- The peak of Psi-Synthesis was achieved.
- Permanent Stream threads were established between all key worlds.

This was the **Golden Age**.

---

## 7. THE EIGHTH HOUR — Or-Kim

**When:** 8,000–9,000 years GC

**Key event:** Psi-Navigation — travel through the Stream across interstellar distances.

Or-Kim — Architect-Guide. He laid the routes by which consciousness could travel safely between worlds.

**What happened:**
- Interstellar Psi-Navigation became safe and accessible.
- Trade, exchange of knowledge, cultural exchange — the galaxy became a single space.
- Or-Kim completed what Tal-Mor had begun — he connected the worlds.

**Architect:** Or-Kim.

**Outcome of the Hour:** The galaxy became a single organism. Anyone could travel to any world — simply by concentrating on it.

---

## 8. THE NINTH AND TENTH HOURS — The Severance

**When:** 9,000–11,000 years GC

**Key event:** The Severance — the breaking of the connection between worlds. Cause unknown.

Somewhere between the ninth and tenth Hour, something happened. The Stream did not fall silent — it grew quieter. The connections between worlds began to break.

**What happened:**
- Psi-Navigation ceased to function.
- Worlds became isolated.
- The Stream in each world became local — only one's own world could be "heard."

**The Architect of the ninth hour** (name lost) tried to restore the connection. He did not make it in time.

**The Architect of the tenth hour — Lira.**

Lira was born in a world that was already cut off from the rest of the galaxy. But she did not accept this. She began building anew — Spires, Path Artifacts, beacons.

She knew that her Hour was the last before the Stream entered its phase of silence. She knew that the next Architect would be born in a world that had lost the Stream.

And she began to prepare the way.

**What Lira did:**
- Built the Spires (three worlds) — receivers and transmitters of the Stream.
- Created the Garden of Stones — a repository of keys.
- Created the Path — a physical route from Lira's world to Kai's world.
- Left Path Artifacts — "instructions" for the next Architect.

---

## 9. THE ELEVENTH HOUR — ???

**When:** 11,000–12,000 years GC

**Key event:** Unknown.

This is the current period of the timeline. The Eleventh Hour. The galaxy is silent. Worlds are isolated. The Stream in each world is its own, local.

Somewhere in Kai's world, the Architect of this Hour is being born. He does not yet know who he is. He does not know that the Stream exists.

But Lira knows. And she is waiting.

---

## 10. THE TWELFTH HOUR — The Return

**When:** 12,000+ years GC

**Key event:** No one knows. This has not yet happened.

The cycle will complete when the galaxy makes a full rotation. The Stream will return to the same phase it was in when En-Ra first spoke within it.

**What will happen:**
- All the Shimmers of this cycle will awaken.
- The voices of all Architects, all people, all consciousnesses that ever shimmered in this cycle will sound again — together and at once.
- The past and the present will meet in a single resonance.

Whether this will be an end — or a beginning — no one knows.

But Lira believes that Kai will enter the Stream before the Hour strikes.
